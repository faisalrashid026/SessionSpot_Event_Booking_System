We’re here to help

A little guidance for your next great experience.

Good questions. Simple answers.

How do I reserve tickets?

Sign in, find a published event, choose ticket quantities and confirm your attendee details. Availability and prices are checked on the server before a booking reference is issued. View the same record in My Bookings.

Can I cancel a reservation?

Open My Bookings, select a confirmed upcoming booking and choose Cancel reservation. Availability is restored once. Past bookings cannot be cancelled.

Do I pay online?

No online payment is collected. Priced tickets show payment due at the venue in Canadian dollars. Free tickets require no payment.

How do I create or manage an event?

Every individual account can create events and reserve tickets. Open Manage events to create drafts, publish events, configure tickets and review actual attendee records. Organization owners can select their organization when creating an event.

How do account access and email work?

Accounts and bookings are saved in the database. Sign in with your own password. There is no institution SSO. Password-reset and contact email require configured delivery; an unavailable email service reports an error rather than claiming a message was sent.

Terms of Service

Reservations follow the event details and may be cancelled before the event starts. Payment for priced tickets is due at the venue. Contact the organizer about event-specific conditions. Local instructor test events do not represent real-world events.

Privacy Policy

Your account, organizations, events and bookings are stored on the server. Passwords are stored as hashes. Session cookies identify signed-in users. Organizers can access attendees for their own events; administrators manage platform records. Contact the operator about data requests.
