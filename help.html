We’re here to help

A little guidance for your next great experience.

Good questions. Simple answers.

How do I reserve tickets?

Find an event, choose your ticket quantities, enter sample attendee details and confirm your demo reservation. You’ll see the same reference in My bookings.

Can I cancel a reservation?

Open My bookings, select a confirmed upcoming booking and choose Cancel reservation. The sample availability updates locally. Past bookings cannot be cancelled.

Do I pay online?

No. This prototype does not collect payment or hold real tickets. Prices are sample amounts in Canadian dollars.

How do I create or manage an event?

Open Manage events or Create an event. Save a sample event, edit its ticket categories and review local sample attendees. All changes stay in your browser.

Are login, SSO and email working services?

No. Account access, institution SSO, contact and password reset are clearly labelled demonstrations. No real email is sent and no password is stored.

Demo terms

This is a university UI prototype, not an event ticketing service. Demo checkboxes are interaction examples, not a service agreement. Use fictional details and do not rely on sample availability.

Demo privacy

Sample events, bookings, roles and profile data stay in this browser’s local storage. Passwords are never stored. Use Settings → Reset sample data to restore the fixtures.
